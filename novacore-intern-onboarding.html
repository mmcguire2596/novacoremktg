Section 01

Understand the Business First

Before creating any content, you need to understand the fundamentals — what each program does, who it serves, and what problems it solves.

💡 We market highly specialized insurance solutions, not generic insurance products. Each program has a different personality, appetite, and audience.
Key questions to always ask
  • 📌 What does this specialty program do?
  • 👥 Who is the audience? (Mostly agents & brokers)
  • 🔧 What problem does this program solve?
  • 📈 Why does this market matter right now?

Brand Voice & Tone Standards

✅ Our Tone Is

  • ✔ Modern
  • ✔ Confident
  • ✔ Industry-smart
  • ✔ Agent-focused
  • ✔ Conversational
  • ✔ Marketing-forward

❌ Our Tone Is NOT

  • ✗ Overly corporate
  • ✗ Generic insurance jargon
  • ✗ Too salesy
  • ✗ Robotic
Important Brand Reminders
Use "Novacore," not NSM
Tagline = "Transforming Insurance"
Avoid em dashes
Avoid "we're back" phrases
Avoid overly negative framing
Don't imply AI replaces underwriters
Punchy copy over long paragraphs

Section 05

Social Media is NOT an Afterthought

🔗 LinkedIn is our primary platform. Every post should feel like it came from an industry expert, not a generic brand account.
LinkedIn Goals
Industry credibility
Program awareness
Executive visibility
Recruiting / culture
Thought leadership
Typical LinkedIn Post Structure
  • 1️⃣ Strong hook — stop the scroll
  • 2️⃣ Short, punchy body copy
  • 3️⃣ One clear CTA
  • 4️⃣ Strategic hashtags
Social Philosophy
  • ✅ Make insurance feel modern
  • ✅ Keep content visually clean
  • ❌ Avoid stock-looking corporate content
  • ✅ Tie content to real market activity
